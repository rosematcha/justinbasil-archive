  <p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;"><strong>Other Restrictions and Rules<br></strong>In addition to the official rules concerning deck construction limitations imposed on the Expanded format (e.g. Professor Sycamore, Professor Juniper, Professor’s Research cannot be in the same deck), there are a few additional deck construction limitations in the Eternal format.</p><ul data-rte-list="default"><li><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">A deck may contain either <a href="https://pkmncards.com/card/crushing-hammer-emerging-powers-epo-92/">Crushing Hammer</a> <em>or </em><a href="https://pkmncards.com/card/energy-removal-2-expedition-ex-140/">Energy Removal 2</a>.</p></li><li><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">A deck may contain up to 4 copies in any combination of the following cards (of any print):</p><ul data-rte-list="default"><li><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;"><a href="https://pkmncards.com/card/clefairy-doll-base-set-bs-70/">Clefairy Doll</a></p></li><li><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;"><a href="https://pkmncards.com/card/mysterious-fossil-fossil-fo-62/">Mysterious Fossil</a></p></li><li><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;"><a href="https://pkmncards.com/card/claw-fossil-sandstorm-ss-90/">Claw Fossil</a></p></li><li><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;"><a href="https://pkmncards.com/card/root-fossil-sandstorm-ss-92/">Root Fossil</a></p></li><li><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;"><a href="https://pkmncards.com/card/robo-substitute-team-flare-gear-phantom-forces-phf-102/">Robo Substitute Team Flare Gear</a></p></li><li><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;"><a href="https://pkmncards.com/card/lillies-poke-doll-cosmic-eclipse-cec-197/">Lillie’s Poké Doll</a></p></li></ul></li><li><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Cards in a deck must always be played with the text of its newest print in mind, including any changes to card designations or other card restrictions. For example, this means that <a href="https://pkmncards.com/card/master-ball-gym-challenge-g2-116/">Master Ball G1 116</a> may be used in place of the Ace Spec <a href="https://pkmncards.com/card/master-ball-plasma-blast-plb-94/">Master Ball</a> card from Plasma Blast, but it must still be treated as an Ace Spec and its effect is now “Search your deck for a Pokémon, reveal it, and put it into your hand. Shuffle your deck afterward.” Follow any <a href="https://assets.pokemon.com/assets/cms/pdf/tcg/tcg_errata.pdf">official errata</a>.</p></li><li><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Card errors do not create distinct prints for the purposes of the ban list. For example, <a href="https://pkmncards.com/card/dark-vileplume-team-rocket-ro-13/">Dark Vileplume TR 13</a> and <a href="https://pkmncards.com/card/dark-vileplume-team-rocket-ro-30/">Dark Vileplume TR 30</a> are the same card, despite the Fighting Weakness error on the non-holo print of the card. Both prints are banned.</p></li></ul><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;"><strong>Understanding Older Cards</strong></p><ul data-rte-list="default"><li><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">For any card effect referring to a Trainer card<em> </em>printed on a card before <em>Diamond &amp; Pearl</em> base set, any sub-class of Trainer card is affected, including Supporter cards, Stadium cards, Pokémon Tool cards, and Trainer cards (<em>Diamond &amp; Pearl / HeartGold &amp; SoulSilver</em> era) or Item cards (<em>Black &amp; White</em> onward). </p></li><li><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Any card printed in or after <em>Black &amp; White</em> base set that refers to an “Item card” and any card printed in the <em>Diamond &amp; Pearl</em> era or <em>HeartGold &amp; SoulSilver</em> era that refers to a “Trainer” card can target any Trainer card printed without a “Supporter,” “Stadium,” or “Pokémon Tool” card designation as if that card were an Item card (provided that the target Trainer card does not have a newer print that designates it as a Supporter, Stadium, or Pokémon Tool card). (See <a href="https://compendium.pokegym.net/ruling/1060/">this Compendium Ruling</a>.) </p></li><li><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Any card from the <em>Diamond &amp; Pearl</em> era or <em>HeartGold &amp; SoulSilver</em> era that is designated as a Supporter, Trainer, or Stadium<em> </em>card can be targeted by the effects of cards printed before <em>Diamond &amp; Pearl </em>base set and in and after <em>Black &amp; White </em>base set that target Trainer cards, even if that card has not been reprinted with the current Trainer card designation. Treat Trainer cards from before <em>Black &amp; White </em>base set not designated as Supporter cards, Stadium cards, or Pokémon Tool cards as Item cards. </p></li><li><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Any cards from the <em>Diamond &amp; Pearl </em>or <em>HeartGold &amp; SoulSilver</em> era that target “Trainer cards” should be read as targeting “Item” cards.</p></li><li><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Even when a Pokémon Tool card printed prior to Scarlet &amp; Violet base set has the word “Item” printed on it, it is not an Item card, per <a href="https://www.pokemon.com/us/pokemon-news/2023-pokemon-tcg-standard-format-rotation-and-pokemon-tool-errata">this errata</a>. Pokémon Tool cards are now a distinct subclass of Trainer card separate from Item cards. Cards that search for Item cards cannot find Pokémon Tool cards unless specifically stated. <em>Diamond &amp; Pearl </em>era cards, including cards from the <em>HeartGold &amp; SoulSilver</em> block, that search for Trainer cards (Item cards) also cannot find Pokémon Tool cards unless specifically stated.</p></li><li><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Any Pokémon printed before <em>Black &amp; White </em>base set that refers to itself by name should be interpreted as saying “this Pokémon.” For example, Gyarados HS 123’s Thrash attack reads, “Flip a coin. If heads, this attack does 30 damage plus 20 more damage. If tails, Gyarados does 20 damage to itself.” This should be interpreted as, “Flip a coin. If heads, this attack does 20 more damage. If tails, this Pokémon does 20 damage to itself.” (In short, follow <a href="https://compendium.pokegym.net/ruling/902/">this Compendium ruling</a>.)</p></li><li><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">When the effect of a card printed before Black &amp; White base set tells you to remove damage counters from your Pokémon, interpret that as to "heal" your Pokémon.   <p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Simon Narode, the list’s creator, introduced the deck as follows:</p><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Have you ever wondered what Pokémon would be like if you stripped away all the RNG and competed on pure in-game skill? Three years ago, I set out to design a deck for that purpose, to play against an identical copy of itself in a perfectly skill-based 60-card mirror match.</p><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">In this game, each player has access to the same suite of options, threats, and counters. Without luck to fall back on, your game-planning, resource-management, and risk-assessment skills become especially important. Even if you’re down five prizes, you can pull out a win with cards like Mystery Plate β and Pow! Hand Extension.</p></li><li><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Other luck elements have also been removed.
