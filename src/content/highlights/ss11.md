**Note**: Alternate art and full art cards from VMAX Climax and Dark Phantasm may appear in this set’s Trainer Gallery. To see the full set of confirmed cards in Lost Origin’s Trainer Gallery and other possible inclusions in the Lost Origin Trainer Gallery, see the [Trainer Galleries article](/visual/tg).

[P] Screaming Circle: Put 2 damage counters on your opponent’s Active Pokémon for each of your opponent’s Benched Pokémon.

[P][C][C] Psychic: 70+ damage. This attack does 20 more damage for each Energy attached to your opponent’s Active Pokémon.

![](/images/b485678b2094468f.webp)

</div></div></div><div class="jb-col-4">

[P][P] Spooky Shot: 50 damage.

[P][C] Spinning Attack: 30 damage.

![](/images/922cfb499e559663.webp)

</div><div class="jb-col-4">

Ability: Guardian of Love Prevent all effects of your opponent’s Pokémon’s Abilities done to each of your Pokémon that has any [P] Energy attached, except any Enamorus V.

[P][C][C] Blossom Tail: 100 damage. Attach up to 2 basic Energy cards from your discard pile to your Benched Pokémon in any way you like.
