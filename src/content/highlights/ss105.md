**Utility Pokémon**

<div class="jb-row"><div class="jb-col-2">

![](/images/pokemon-tcg-pokemon-go-venusaur-3.webp)

</div><div class="jb-col-4">

**Venusaur**

Ability: Loopy Lasso

Once during your turn, you may flip a coin. if heads, switch 1 of your opponent’s Benched Pokémon with their Active Pokémon, and the new Active Pokémon is now Asleep and Poisoned.

[G][G][C][C] Solar Beam: 130 damage.

</div><div class="jb-col-2">

![](/images/pokemon-tcg-pokemon-go-radiant-venusaur-4.webp)

</div><div class="jb-col-4">

**Radiant Venusaur**

Ability: Sunny Bloom

Once at the end of your turn <em>(after your attack)</em>, you may use this Ability. Draw cards until you have 4 cards in your hand.

[G][G][C] Pollen Hazard: 90 damage. Your opponent’s Active Pokémon is now Burned, Confused, and Poisoned.

</div></div><div class="jb-row"><div class="jb-col-2">

![](/images/pokemon-tcg-pokemon-go-charizard-10.webp)

</div><div class="jb-col-10"><div class="jb-row"><div class="jb-col-4">

**Charizard**

Ability: Burn Brightly Each basic [R] Energy attached to your Pokémon provides [R][R] Energy. You can’t apply more than 1 Burn Brightly Ability at a time.

[R][R][C][C] Flare Blitz: 170 damage. Discard all [R] Energy from this Pokémon.

</div><div class="jb-col-6"><div class="jb-row"><div class="jb-col-2">

![](/images/pokemon-tcg-pokemon-go-blastoise-17.webp)

</div><div class="jb-col-4">

**Blastoise**

Ability: Vitality Spring

Once during your turn, you may search your deck for up to 6 Energy cards and attach them to your Pokémon in any way you like. Then, shuffle your deck. If you use this Ability, your turn ends.

[C][C][C][C] Hydro Pump: 90+ damage. This attack does 30 more damage for each [W] Energy attached to this Pokémon.

</div></div></div></div></div></div><div class="jb-row"><div class="jb-col-2">

![](/images/pokemon-tcg-pokemon-go-radiant-blastoise-18.webp)

</div><div class="jb-col-4">

**Radiant Blastoise**

Ability: Pump Shot You must discard a [W] Energy card from your hand in order to use this Ability. Once during your turn, you may put 2 damage counters on 1 of your opponent’s Benched Pokémon.

[W][W][C] Torrential Cannon: 170 damage. During your next turn, this Pokémon can’t use Torrential Cannon.

</div><div class="jb-col-2">

![](/images/pokemon-tcg-pokemon-go-snorlax-55.webp)

</div><div class="jb-col-4">

**Snorlax**

Ability: Block As long as this Pokémon is in the Active Spot, your opponent’s Active Pokémon can’t retreat.

[C][C][C][C] Collapse: 150 damage. This Pokémon is now Asleep.

*VSTAR Power* [P][C] Star Raid: This attack does 120 damage to each of your opponent’s Pokémon V. This damage isn’t affected by Weakness or Resistance. <em>(You can’t use more than 1 VSTAR Power in a game.)</em>

[C][C][C] Super Fang: Put damage counters on your opponent’s Active Pokémon until its remaining HP is 10.

Ability: Sudden Transformation This Pokémon can use the attacks of any Basic Pokémon in your discard pile, except for Pokémon with a Rule Box <em>(Pokémon V, Pokémon-GX, etc. have Rule Boxes.) (You still need the necessary Energy to use each attack.</em>

**Items**

<div class="jb-row"><div class="jb-col-2">

![](/images/pokemon-tcg-pokemon-go-lure-module-67.webp)

</div><div class="jb-col-4">

**Lure Module**

Each player reveals the top 3 cards of their deck and puts all Pokémon they find there into their hand. Then, each player shuffles the other cards back into their deck.

</div><div class="jb-col-6"><div class="jb-row"><div class="jb-col-2">

![](/images/171-7.webp)

</div><div class="jb-col-4">

**Rare Candy**

Choose 1 of your Basic Pokémon in play. If you have a Stage 2 card in your hand that evolves from that Pokémon, put that card on the Basic Pokémon to evolve it, skipping the Stage 1. You can't use this card during your first turn or on a Basic Pokémon that was put into play this turn.

**Everything Else**

<div class="jb-row"><div class="jb-col-2">

![](/images/pokemon-tcg-pokemon-go-pokestop-68.webp)

</div><div class="jb-col-4">

**PokéStop**

Once during each player’s turn, that player may discard 3 cards from the top of their deck. If a player discarded any Item cards in this way, they put those Item cards into their hand.

</div><div class="jb-col-6"></div></div>
