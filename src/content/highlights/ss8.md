**Utility Pokémon**

<div class="jb-row"><div class="jb-col-2">

</div><div class="jb-col-4">

**Oricorio**

Ability: Lesson in Zeal All of your Fusion Strike Pokémon take 20 less damage from attacks from your opponent’s Pokémon <em>(after applying Weakness and Resistance)</em>. You can’t apply more than 1 Lesson in Zeal Ability at a time.

[R][C] Glistening Droplets: Put 5 damage counters on your opponent’s Pokémon in any way you like.

</div><div class="jb-col-6"><div class="jb-row"><div class="jb-col-2">

![](/images/166-4.webp)

</div><div class="jb-col-4">

**Huntail**

Ability: Single Strike Jammer Your opponent’s Single Strike Pokémon’s attacks cost [C] more.

[W][C][C] Cavernous Chomp: 80 damage.

</div></div></div></div><div class="jb-row"><div class="jb-col-2">

![](/images/167-4.webp)

</div><div class="jb-col-10"><div class="jb-row"><div class="jb-col-4">

**Gorebyss**

Ability: Rapid Strike Canceler Your opponent’s Rapid Strike Pokémon in play have no Abilities.

[W][C] Draining Kiss: 50 damage. Heal 30 damage from this Pokémon.

</div><div class="jb-col-2">

![](/images/179-4.webp)

</div><div class="jb-col-4">

**Inteleon VMAX**

Ability: Double Gunner You must discard a [W] Energy card from your hand in order to use this Ability. Once during your turn, you may choose 2 of your opponent’s Benched Pokémon and put 2 damage counters on each of them.

[W][C] G-Max Spiral: 70+ damage. You may put an Energy attached to this Pokémon into your hand. If you do, this attack does 70 more damage.

</div></div></div></div><div class="jb-row"><div class="jb-col-2">

![](/images/1108-1.webp)

</div><div class="jb-col-4">

**Toxtricity**

Ability: Maximum Downer If all of your Pokéon in play are Fusion Strike Pokémon, your opponent’s Pokémon VMAX in play get -30 HP.

[L][L][C] Head Bolt: 90 damage.

</div><div class="jb-col-2">

![](/images/1116-1.webp)

</div><div class="jb-col-4">

**Granbull**

Ability: Dig Up When you play this Pokémon from your hand to evolve 1 of your Pokémon during your turn, you may put up 2 to Pokémon Tool cards from your discard pile into your hand.

[C][C][C] Bite: 90 damage.

</div></div><div class="jb-row"><div class="jb-col-2">

![](/images/1185-2.webp)

</div><div class="jb-col-4">

**Genesect V**

Ability: Fusion Strike System Once during your turn, you may draw cards until you have as many cards in your hand as you have Fusion Strike Pokémon.

[M][M][C] Techno Blast: 210 damage. During your next turn, this Pokémon can’t attack.

</div><div class="jb-col-2">

![](/images/1207-2.webp)

</div><div class="jb-col-4">

**Dunsparce**

Ability: Mysterious Nest [C] Pokémon in play <em>(both yours and your opponent’s)</em> have no Weakness.

[C][C] Rollout: 30 damage.

![](/images/170-4.webp)

![](/images/1114-1-11d192.webp)

[P][C][C] Spooky Shot: 80 damage.

![](/images/1120-1.webp)

This card may only be attached to Fusion Strike Pokémon. If it’s attached to a Pokémon that isn’t a Fusion Strike Pokémon, discard this card.
