**Mew ex**

Ability: Restart Once during your turn, you may draw cards until you have 3 cards in your hand.

[C][C][C] Genome Hacking: Choose 1 of your opponent’s Active Pokémon’s attacks and use it as this attack.

</div><div class="jb-col-6"></div></div><div class="jb-code-container">

**Attacking Pokémon**

<div class="jb-row"><div class="jb-col-2">

![](/images/sv3-5-en-009.webp)

</div><div class="jb-col-4">

**Blastoise ex**

Ability: Solid Shell This Pokémon takes 30 less damage from attacks <em>(after applying Weakness and Resistance)</em>.

[W][W] Twin Cannons: 140x damage. Discard up to 2 Basic [W] Energy cards from your hand. This attack does 140 damage for each card you discarded in this way.

</div><div class="jb-col-2">

![](/images/sv3-5-en-065.webp)

</div><div class="jb-col-4">

**Alakazam ex**

[C][C] Mind Jack: 90+ damage. This attack does 30 more damage for each of your opponent’s Benched Pokémon.

[P][P] Dimensional Manipulation: 120 damage. This Pokémon can use this attack even from the Bench.

</div></div><div class="jb-code-container">

</div>
</div>

**Supporters**

<div class="jb-row"><div class="jb-col-2">

![](/images/sv3-5-en-160.webp)

</div><div class="jb-col-4">

**Erika’s Invitation**

Your opponent reveals their hand, and you put a Basic Pokémon from your opponent’s discard pile onto your opponent’s Bench. if you put a Pokémon onto their Bench in this way, switch in that Pokémon to the Active Spot.

</div><div class="jb-col-2">

![](/images/sv3-5-en-161.webp)

</div><div class="jb-col-4">

**Giovanni’s Charisma**

Put an Energy attached to your opponent’s Active Pokémon into their hand. If you do, attach an Energy card from your hand to your Active Pokémon.

![](/images/sv3-5-en-162.webp)

**Grabber**

Your opponent reveals their hand, and you put a Pokémon you find there on the bottom of their deck.
