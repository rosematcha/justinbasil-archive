A new Hall of Fame page has been created for the alternate format tournaments held each month on the [JustInBasil Discord server](https://discord.gg/gy52nzras2). On this page, you’ll see the results of past alternate format tournaments, including the deck lists for the top 4 players from each tournament!

Visit the new [Alternate Formats Hall of Fame page](/league/halloffame) and join us each month to potentially get a spot in the Hall of Fame!
