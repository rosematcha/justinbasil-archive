Ban list updates are now live for two alternate formats. Please keep in mind the new JIB Format ban list when designing your decks for the upcoming JIB Format tournament on May 6th.

### Aceless

The following card has been added to the Aceless format ban list: Malamar CRE 70.

### JIB Format

For the first time since its creation, two cards have been added to the ban list of the JIB Format (currently JIB1 and JIB Promos JIB001 through JIB011).

Going forward, Professor’s Research JIB1 141 and Quick Ball JIB1 142 are banned in the JIB format.
