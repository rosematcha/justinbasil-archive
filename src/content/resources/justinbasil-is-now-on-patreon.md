You can now [support JustInBasil on Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/justinbasil)! As of now, there are four tiers available to choose from with the following small perks:

<strong>Fresh Basilites ($5/mo.)</strong> - Access to a Patron-exclusive Discord channel on the JustInBasil Discord server - News about upcoming updates to justinbasil.com

<strong>Basil Brigade ($10/mo.)</strong> - Benefits of the previous tier plus: - Early previews of in-development resources

<strong>Pesto Patriots ($25/mo.)</strong> - Benefits of the previous tiers plus: - A special thanks in the “Patreon” bubble on the home page of justinbasil.com

<strong>Margherita Masters ($50/mo.)</strong> - All of the benefits of the previous tiers, and the opportunity to go head to head with me each month in a best of five games on PTCGO in a format of your choosing (Standard, Expanded, or one of the special [League at Home formats](/league/formats)).

Of course, no matter which tier you choose, you’ll have my genuine thanks and appreciation! Donating is, of course, completely optional and any content that would ordinarily be hosted on justinbasil.com will be continue to be hosted here—it won’t be locked behind any sort of “Patreon paywall.” Patreon is just a way for those who are interested in donating to do so—with small perks that won’t distract too much from the work that is needed to maintain this site.
