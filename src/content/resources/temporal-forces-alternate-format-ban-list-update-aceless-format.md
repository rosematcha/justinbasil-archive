Effective 5 April 2024, the following cards, and any alternate printings they may have, are banned in their respective formats.

**In the Aceless Format**

- [Machamp MEW 68](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/MEW/68)
- [Brambleghast TEF 21](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/TEF/21)

Brambleghast below is represented by its English-language proxy below as the card does not officially release until March 22nd.

Also of note: Temporal Forces reintroduces *ACE SPEC* cards into the Standard format. However, *ACE SPEC* cards are **not** legal in the Aceless format. It is, after all, Aceless!
