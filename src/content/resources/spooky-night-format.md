In a little celebration of Halloween, have some fun building around some of Pokémon’s spookiest monsters!

Restrictions:

- No Rule Box Pokémon.
- Cards from SUM-onward are legal, as are prior prints of cards in a legal set.
- The following cards and any alternate prints of them are banned:
  - [Chip-Chip Ice Axe](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/UNB/165)
  - [Dragapult RCL 91](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/RCL/91)
  - [Dusk Stone](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/UNB/167)
  - [Jessie & James](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/HIF/58)
  - [Lt. Surge’s Strategy](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/UNB/178)
  - [Malamar CRE 70](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/CRE/70)
  - [Marshadow SLG 45](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/SLG/45)
  - [Reset Stamp](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/UNM/206)
