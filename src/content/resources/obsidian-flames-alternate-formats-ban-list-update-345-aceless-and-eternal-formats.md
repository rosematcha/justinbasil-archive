*Illus. Hitoshi Ariga*



Effective 11 August 2023, the following cards, and any alternate printings they may have, are banned in their respective formats.

**In the 345 Format**

- [Hariyama FST 143](https://www.limitlesstcg.com/cards/FST/143)

**In the Aceless Format**

- [Hariyama FST 143](https://www.limitlesstcg.com/cards/FST/143)

Also effective 11 August 2023, the following cards, and any alternate printings they may have, are no longer banned in their respective formats.

**In the Eternal Format**

- [Max Elixir](https://pkmncards.com/card/max-elixir-breakpoint-bkp-102/)
- [Electric Generator](https://pkmncards.com/card/electric-generator-scarlet-violet-svi-170/)
