All of the following ban list updates below are effective immediately.

**345 Format**

Two cards have been added to the ban list for the 345 Format: [Dewgong LOR 34](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/LOR/34) and [Machamp LOR 88](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/LOR/88).

**Eternal Format**

Two cards have been added to the ban list for the Eternal Format: [Lost Blender LOT 181](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/LOT/181) and [Green’s Exploration UNB 175](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/UNB/175).
