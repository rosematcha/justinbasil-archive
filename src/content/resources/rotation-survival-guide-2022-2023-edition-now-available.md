With the upcoming rotation looming, it’s time to start thinking about which cards survive the upcoming Standard format rotation and which cards will not. This year’s rotation will likely be announced in mid-late April, with rotation itself happening in August or September of this year.

Four of the five articles that make up [this year’s Rotation Survival Guide](/rotation/e-on) are available now, including:

- [Introduction to Rotation](/rotation/e-on/introduction)
- [Noteworthy Losses and Retentions (SSH-SHF)](/rotation/e-on/noteworthy-losses-and-retentions)
- [The New Essentials](/rotation/e-on/essentials)
- [2023 Rotation Card Status (SSH-SHF)](/rotation/e-on/card-status)

As we get closer to the release of *Lost Origin* and rotation itself, the Archetypes to Consider after Rotation article will also become available.
