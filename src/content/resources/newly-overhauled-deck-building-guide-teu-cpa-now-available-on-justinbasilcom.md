The newly-overhauled version of the *Basic Guide to Deck Building in the Standard Format* has been made available prior to full completion so that new deck builders for the Pokémon Trading Card Game can once again have a handy, relevant deck building resource at their fingertips.

This new guide features an all new structure to make it easier to follow and more useful, is more mobile-friendly, and includes many new pages not found in the previous version of the guide!

A handful of pages in the guide are not yet complete, but the guide in its current state is now more useful than the previous, out-of-date version of the guide. Please be patient as the remainder of the guide is completed!
