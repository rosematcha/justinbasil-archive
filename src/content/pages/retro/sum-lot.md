    <h2>Lost March</h2>
     <span></span>
    </div>
</div></div><div class="jb-row"><div class="jb-col-6">

![](/images/lost-march.webp)

*Illus. Masakazu Fukuda*



### BASIC STRATEGY

Use Professor Elm’s Lecture to setup Jumpluff, using Skiploom’s Floral Path to the Sky ability to send the Hoppip and Skiploom to the Lost Zone to boost Jumpluff’s Lost March attack. Further boost Lost March’s damage with Trumbeak’s Mountain Pass ability and by using Lost Blender to put extra Pokémon into the Lost Zone. Use Natu’s Lost March attack to hit for weakness against Psychic-weak Pokémon.
