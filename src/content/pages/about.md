Either Sylveon or Umbreon, depending on when you ask me. I also really like Leafeon.</p><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;"><strong>Q. Who is your favorite gym leader?</strong></p><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">A. Erika. Which generation has been your favorite? </strong></p><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">A. I’ve loved all of the games for various reasons, but I think it’s fair to say that generation II has been my favorite. I loved Johto and the twist that Gold and Silver brought to the formula that Red and Blue started. HeartGold and SoulSilver only made Johto even better. I hope that one day Johto can get a game all on its own, without incorporating Kanto, so that it can be more fully realized and explored in the same way that the regions from other generations have enjoyed. That aside, my favorite recent games are definitely Pokémon Scarlet and Pokémon Violet. These games were a massive transitional step for the franchise and I had an absolute blast playing Pokémon Violet, even with its rough edges.</p><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;"><strong>Q.
