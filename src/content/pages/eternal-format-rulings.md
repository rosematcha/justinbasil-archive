  <h1 style="padding-bottom:0px; margin-bottom:0px; margin-top: 3px;">Attack-Copying Questions</h1>

<div class="sqs-html-content" data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Q. Can Mew TM 97’s Poké-Body Lost Link copy the attacks of Pokémon LEGEND, Pokémon BREAK, or Pokémon V-UNION in the Lost Zone? For example, can Lost Link copy Raticate BREAK BKP 89’s Super Fang attack for [C][C] when Raticate BREAK BKP 89 is in the Lost Zone?</p><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">A. No, it cannot. Pokémon BREAK, Pokémon LEGEND, and Pokémon V-UNION are not complete Pokémon when not in play and thus cannot have their attacks copied by Lost Link. (<a href="https://compendium.pokegym.net/ruling/163/">Official Ruling for Pokémon LEGEND</a>.)</p>
</div>

<div class="sqs-html-content" data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Q. Can Mew TM 97’s Poké-Body Lost Link copy the attacks of Pokémon LV.X in the Lost Zone? For example, can Lost Link copy Luxray GL LV.X RR 109’s Flash Impact attack for [L][C] when Luxray GL LV.X RR 109 is in the Lost Zone?</p><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">A. Yes, it can, provided you fulfill the attack’s Energy requirements. (Official ruling can be located in the <a href="https://compendium.pokegym.net/compendium-lvx.html">Compendium LV.X</a>.)</p>
</div>

<div class="sqs-html-content" data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Q. Can Alakazam Star’s Skill Copy attack copy the attacks of a discarded Pokémon LEGEND, Pokémon BREAK, or Pokémon V-UNION? For example, can Skill Copy copy Yanmega BREAK BKP 89’s Barrier Break attack for [C][C][C] when Yanmega BREAK STS 8 is discarded?</p><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">A. No, it cannot. Pokémon BREAK, Pokémon LEGEND, and Pokémon V-UNION are not complete Pokémon when not in play and thus cannot have their attacks copied by Alakazam Star. (See <a href="https://pokegym.net/community/index.php?threads/alakazam.177751/post-2445754">this PokéGym Forum post</a>.)</p>
</div>




















  
  



</div></div><div class="sqs-block horizontalrule-block sqs-block-horizontalrule" data-block-type="47" data-sqsp-block="line" id="block-yui_3_17_2_1_1719159092112_32516"><div class="sqs-block-content"><hr></div></div><div class="sqs-block html-block sqs-block-html" data-block-type="2" data-sqsp-block="text" id="block-yui_3_17_2_1_1719159092112_32829"><div class="sqs-block-content">

<div class="sqs-html-content" data-sqsp-text-block-content="">

For example, can Skill Copy copy Luxray GL LV.X RR 109’s Flash Impact attack for [L][C] when Luxray GL LV.X RR 109 is discarded?</p><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">A. Yes, you can, despite Pokémon LV.X being incomplete Pokémon. (See <a href="https://pokegym.net/community/index.php?threads/alakazam-star-and-level-x.67584/" target="_blank">this PokéGym Forum post.</a>)</p>

<div class="sqs-html-content" data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Q. In the Eternal format, how do Unown from the Diamond &amp; Pearl era and Unown from the Neo era interact? For example, does Unown [E] N2 67 count as an “Unown E” for the purposes of Unown [S]’s SET Poké-Power? </p><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">A. Yes. Any Unown with a letter in its name (as printed on the card) will count as such. However, Unown without the letter in their name, like Unown LOT 92, do <em>not </em>count for such a purpose since the Unown’s letter is not part of the name.</p>
</div>




















  
  



</div></div><div class="sqs-block horizontalrule-block sqs-block-horizontalrule" data-block-type="47" data-sqsp-block="line" id="block-yui_3_17_2_1_1674483958178_14731"><div class="sqs-block-content"><hr></div></div><div class="sqs-block html-block sqs-block-html" data-block-type="2" data-border-radii="{&quot;topLeft&quot;:{&quot;unit&quot;:&quot;px&quot;,&quot;value&quot;:0.0},&quot;topRight&quot;:{&quot;unit&quot;:&quot;px&quot;,&quot;value&quot;:0.0},&quot;bottomLeft&quot;:{&quot;unit&quot;:&quot;px&quot;,&quot;value&quot;:0.0},&quot;bottomRight&quot;:{&quot;unit&quot;:&quot;px&quot;,&quot;value&quot;:0.0}}" data-sqsp-block="text" id="block-yui_3_17_2_1_1674483958178_14791"><div class="sqs-block-content">

<div class="sqs-html-content" data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Q. The Unown from the Neo era have the text “You may have up to 4 Basic Pokémon cards in your deck with Unown in their names.” printed on them. How does this work with other Unown in the Eternal format?</p><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">A. If any of the Unown in your deck have this rule printed on them, then the rule applies to your deck. You are limited to a total of 4 Basic Pokémon with “Unown” in their name. (See <a href="https://pokegym.net/community/index.php?threads%2Funown-in-unlimited.186166%2F#post-2508874">this PokéGym Forum post</a>.)</p>
</div>




















  
  



</div></div><div class="sqs-block horizontalrule-block sqs-block-horizontalrule" data-block-type="47" data-sqsp-block="line" id="block-yui_3_17_2_1_1674483958178_15369"><div class="sqs-block-content"><hr></div></div><div class="sqs-block html-block sqs-block-html" data-block-type="2" data-border-radii="{&quot;topLeft&quot;:{&quot;unit&quot;:&quot;px&quot;,&quot;value&quot;:0.0},&quot;topRight&quot;:{&quot;unit&quot;:&quot;px&quot;,&quot;value&quot;:0.0},&quot;bottomLeft&quot;:{&quot;unit&quot;:&quot;px&quot;,&quot;value&quot;:0.0},&quot;bottomRight&quot;:{&quot;unit&quot;:&quot;px&quot;,&quot;value&quot;:0.0}}" data-sqsp-block="text" id="block-yui_3_17_2_1_1674483958178_15657"><div class="sqs-block-content">

<div class="sqs-html-content" data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Q. Can I evolve Unown [V] LA 79 or Unown [V] N4 89 into Unown VSTAR SIT 66?</p><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">A. No, you can’t. There’s an <a href="https://compendium.pokegym.net/ruling/1798/">official ruling</a> on this specific situation. In short, the Unown [V] that aren’t the Unown V from Silver Tempest are not “Unown V” but “Unown [V]” and are treated as having a different name from Unown V. As such, they cannot evolve into Unown VSTAR, which evolves only from “Unown V.”</p>
</div>

  <p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Q. For the purposes of card effects, how do new and old Pokémon ex interact with one another and with cards from one another’s eras? <div class="sqs-html-content" data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Q. The text of Nightly Garbage Run from Team Rocket says, “Choose up to 3 Basic Pokémon cards, Evolution cards, and/or basic Energy cards from your discard pile. Show them to your opponent and shuffle them into your deck.” With this wording, can I shuffle back a Baby Pokémon, a Pokémon, LV.X or a Pokémon V-UNION into my deck?</p><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">A. Yes, you can. At the start of the Diamond &amp; Pearl block, the older phrase “Basic Pokémon or Evolution card” was changed to “Pokémon” and cards that use the older phrasing should be treated as though they say “Pokémon” in place of “Basic Pokémon or Evolution card.” So, in this example, interpret Nightly Garbage Run as if it says, “Choose up to 3 Pokémon cards and/or Basic Energy cards from your discard pile. Show them to your opponent and shuffle them into your deck.” See also <a href="https://pokegym.net/community/index.php?threads%2Fdiamond-pearl-rules-updates-and-tourney-impacts-info-released.52159%2F" target="_blank">this post on the rules changes</a> made at the start of the Diamond &amp; Pearl era and <a href="https://compendium.pokegym.net/compendium-lvx.html#14" target="_blank">this ruling</a> from the Compendium LV.X regarding how the card Celio’s Network works after the change.</p>
