<h1 class="jb-center">What to Play -&nbsp;Expanded Meta Decks</h1>

<h3 class="jb-center">Meta Update Page Decommissioned 14 May 2023</h3>



---



Because of a lack of official support for the Expanded format from the Pokémon Company International, which has led to a severe lack of meaningful results for the Expanded format, monthly Expanded-format meta updates are on indefinite hiatus.

You can get a snapshot of the meta from the few small online events that do take place via [Play Limitless](https://play.limitlesstcg.com/decks?format=expanded).

Should the format again receive official support such that meaningful data can again be collected, the *What to Play – Expanded Meta* page will return in the future.
