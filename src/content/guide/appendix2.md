<h2 class="jb-center-top0">In the Standard format</h2>
<h3 class="jb-center-top0">2024 (F Regulation Onward)</h3>
</div>

Noteworthy cards from each set in the Standard format have been split into three categories below. **Staple Cards** are the most important cards in a set and will see frequent use in a variety of decks or in high-performing top tier decks. **Set Highlights** are important cards from the set that won’t show up in every deck but are important for specific decks and **Other Noteworthy Cards** are cards that have or may have some use in specific situations or decks. For a list of *only* the most essential cards for deck builders, visit the article [The New Essentials](/rotation/f-on/essentials).
