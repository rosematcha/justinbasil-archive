<div class="sqs-html-content" data-sqsp-text-block-content="">
  <p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Noteworthy cards from each set in the Standard format have been split into three categories below. <strong>Staple Cards</strong> are the most important cards in a set and will see frequent use in a variety of decks or in high-performing top tier decks. <strong>Set Highlights </strong>are important cards from the set that won’t show up in every deck but are important for specific decks and <strong>Other Noteworthy Cards </strong>are cards that have or may have some use in specific situations or decks. For a list of <em>only </em>the most essential cards for deck builders, visit the article <a href="/rotation/f-on/essentials" target="">The New Essentials</a>.</p>
