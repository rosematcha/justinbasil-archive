<h1 class="jb-center">Prize Pack Series 4 Set List</h1>



![](/images/06eaf1fad6c59628.webp)



Below is a visual set list for cards contained in Prize Pack Series 4. In the [text-based set list below](#textlist) the visual set list, each card is marked S (available in non-holo), H (available in cosmos holofoil), or both. Although not pictured below, each card is stamped with the Play! Pokémon logo.

Prize Pack Series 4 officially releases on Wednesday, February 14th and cards first printed with the Play! Pokémon stamp in either holo or non-holo form are not tournament legal until that date. You can also download an official checklist for Prize Pack Series 4 [here](https://www.pokemon.com/static-assets/content-assets/cms2/pdf/trading-card-game/checklist/prize_pack_series_4_web_cardlist_en.pdf).
