Set Abbreviation: ASR

### **Other set details**

Astral Radiance is comprised of cards from the twin Japanese sets Time Gazer and Space Juggler (8 April 2022, 67 cards each). Some cards from the Lucario VSTAR and Darkrai VSTAR Starter Sets (25 February 2022, approximately 14 new cards) also make an appearance in the set, as do many cards from the Japanese set Battle Region (25 February 2022, 67 cards before secret rares).

Note that for this set, the [press release](https://press.pokemon.com/en/releases/MEDIA-ALERT-New-Pokemon-Trading-Card-Game-Sword-ShieldAstral-Rad-39335) and the [official set page](https://www.pokemon.com/us/pokemon-tcg/sword-shield-astral-radiance/explore-and-watch/) originally contradicted one another in terms of card count for Pokémon V and Pokémon VSTAR. The press release said 8 Pokémon VSTAR and 21 Pokémon V, whereas the set page said 7 Pokémon VSTAR and 19 Pokémon V. It appears that one was counting non-base rarities in its total, like alternate or full arts.
