</div></div></div><div class="jb-col-5"></div></div>

It is uncertain when and how we will be getting the new cards from the New Family Box Set which releases 9 July 2021 in Japan, though it is probable that our Fusion Strike set will feature many of these cards. Like the first Family Box Set that later became our first Battle Academy product, the product features three very basic beginner-centric decks designed to help children and their families learn to play the Pokémon Trading Card Game. It seems likely that in addition to seeing these cards in our regular sets, this product will also see an English release similar to the first Battle Academy product.
