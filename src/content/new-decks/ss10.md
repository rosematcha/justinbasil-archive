With the arrival of Astral Radiance this May, we’ll have a handful of new deck archetypes joining the fray. As always, some deck archetypes are better than others—some are meme tier and some are merely playable, fun decks without much competitive promise. Lists below are very rough drafts and are intended as a starting place for testing, not as tournament-ready deck lists. Consider [creating and using proxies to test cards you don’t already own](/guide/proxies) prior to making any purchases.

    <h2>Vespiquen</h2>
    <div class="comp0">
     <span></span>
    </div>
</div>
</div><div class="jb-row"><div class="jb-col-6">

![](/images/vespiquen.webp)

*Illus. Yuya Oka*



### BASIC STRATEGY

Evolve Combee into Vespiquen and use Drizzile and Inteleon’s Shady Dealings ability to search out your copies of Sweet Honey from your deck. Protect your hand with Milotic’s Dew Guard. Attack using Vespiquen’s Honey Rush attack which does 60 damage for each Sweet Honey your reveal from your hand.

### KEY CARDS

- [Vespiquen ASR 12](/highlights/ss10#block-yui_3_17_2_1_1645192700236_28304)
- [Milotic EVS 38](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/evs/38)
- [Sweet Honey](/highlights/ss10#block-yui_3_17_2_1_1645192700236_419634)

### OTHER CONSIDERATIONS

- [Schoolgirl](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/fst/239) and [Schoolboy](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/fst/238) as options for straight draw, making it easier to avoid interrupting your own hand by shuffling it into your deck or needing to discard cards.

    <div class="comp2">
     <span></span>

</div>
</div><div class="jb-row"><div class="jb-col-6">

![](/images/h-lilligant-vstar.webp)

*Illus. 5ban Graphics*



### BASIC STRATEGY

Evolve Hisuian Lilligant V into Hisuian Lilligant VSTAR and use Drizzile and Inteleon’s Shady Dealings ability to search out critical cards, like Gardenia’s Vigor, from your deck. Attack using Hisuian Lilligant VSTAR’s Parallel Spin attack, which does 130 damage plus 100 more damage if you put an Energy attached to Hisuian Lilligant VSTAR into your hand. Use Inteleon’s Quick Shooting ability and Choice Belt to bring down Pokémon with higher HP or use Echoing Horn and Boss’s Orders to pick on vulnerable bench sitters. As needed, attack instead with Zarude V and its Jungle Rage attack, which does 120 damage plus 120 more if the opponent’s Active Pokémon is a Pokémon V.

    <h2>Zarude V</h2>
    <div class="comp1">
     <span></span>
    </div>
</div>
</div><div class="jb-row"><div class="jb-col-6">

![](/images/zarude.webp)

*Illus. 5ban Grapics*



### BASIC STRATEGY

Attach an Energy to Zarude V and begin softening your opponent’s Pokémon with its Leap to Leap attack, which does 30 damage plus 30 damage to one of your opponent’s nenched Pokémon. Use Drizzile and Inteleon’s Shady Dealings ability to search out critical cards, like Gardenia’s Vigor, from your deck. Take knockouts using Zarude V’s Jungle Rage attack, which does 120 damage plus 120 more damage if your opponent’s Active Pokémon is a Pokémon V (including VMAX, VSTAR, and V-UNION). Use Inteleon’s Quick Shooting ability and Choice Belt to bring down Pokémon with higher HP or use Boss’s Orders to pick on vulnerable bench sitters. Use Castform as a Level Ball-searchable pivot Pokémon to ensure that you’re able to attach to a Benched Zarude V using Gardenia’s Vitality.

### KEY CARDS

- [Zarude V BRS 16](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/brs/16)
- [Inteleon CRE 43](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/CRE/43)
- [Gardenia’s Vigor](/highlights/ss10#block-yui_3_17_2_1_1648377543535_129757)

### OTHER CONSIDERATIONS

- [Eldegoss V RCL 19](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/RCL/19) to recycle Boss’s Orders or Gardenia’s Vitality
- [Kricketune ASR 10](/highlights/ss10#block-yui_3_17_2_1_1648377543535_667286) and/or [Cape of Toughness](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/daa/160) to make it more difficult to knockout Zarude V.
- [Shaymin V BRS 13](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/BRS/13), and potentially [Shaymin VSTAR BRS 14](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/BRS/14) as late-game attackers, with Shaymin VSTAR’s Star Bloom VSTAR Power as an option to heal 120 damage from each of your Benched Grass-type Pokémon.
- [Hisuian Lilligant V ASR 17](/highlights/ss10#block-yui_3_17_2_1_1648377543535_1445887) with [Hisuian Lilligant VSTAR ASR 18](/highlights/ss10#block-yui_3_17_2_1_1648377543535_1473399) to have the option to use Hisuian Lilligant VSTAR’s Star Perfume VSTAR Power, which allows you to search out 5 in any combination Grass Pokémon and Grass Energy.
- [Manaphy BRS 41](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/BRS/41) to shield your vulnerable bench sitters, like Sobble.

    <div class="comp3">
     <span></span>
    </div>
</div>
</div><div class="jb-row"><div class="jb-col-6">

![](/images/h-samurott-vstar.webp)

*Illus. 5ban Graphics*



### BASIC STRATEGY

Prepare Hisuian Samurott V by evolving it into Hisuian Samurott VSTAR and using Dark Patch and manual attachments to power up its Merciless Blade attack, which does 110 damage plus 110 more damage if there are any damage counters on your opponent’s Active Pokémon. Get damage counters on your opponent’s Active Pokémon using either Galarian Zigzagoon’s Headbutt Tantrum ability or Hisuian Samurott VSTAR’s Moon-Cutting Star VSTAR Power, which as an ability, puts 4 damage counters on one of your opponent’s Pokémon. Boost your attack’s damage with Choice Belt.

### KEY CARDS

- [Hisuian Samurott VSTAR ASR 102](/highlights/ss10#block-yui_3_17_2_1_1645192700236_300018)
- [Galarian Zigzagoon SSH 117](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/SSH/117)

### OTHER CONSIDERATIONS
