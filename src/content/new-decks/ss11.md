With the arrival of Lost Origin this September, we’ll have a handful of new deck archetypes joining the fray. As always, some deck archetypes are better than others—some are meme tier and some are merely playable, fun decks without much competitive promise. Lists below are very rough drafts and are intended as a starting place for testing, not as tournament-ready deck lists. Consider [creating and using proxies to test cards you don’t already own](/guide/proxies) prior to making any purchases.

::::deck{id="helectrodev" name="Hisuian Electrode V" image="/images/h-electrode-v.webp" illus="5ban Graphics" comp="casual"}

### BASIC STRATEGY

Using a combination of draw options, like Bibarel’s Industrious Incisors ability, and search options, like Peony, seek out cards that ensure Hisuian Electrode will be afflicted by two or more Special Conditions when attacking with its Tantrum Blast attack, which does 100 damage for each Special Condition on Hisuian Electrode V. Apply Special Conditions with Spicy Seasoned Curry and Parasect’s Lethargy Spores Ability, then use Windup Arm to ensure that Hisuian Electrode V can attack, even while affected by the Asleep Special Condition.

### KEY CARDS

- [Hisuian Electrode V 172](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/jp/S10a/78?translate=en)
- [Parasect LOR 5](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/jp/S10a/2?translate=en)
- [Spicy Seasoned Curry](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/ASR/151)
- [Windup Arm](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/jp/S10a/65?translate=en)

### OTHER CONSIDERATIONS

- [Canceling Cologne](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/ASR/136) to help with the Miltank matchup.

::::deck{id="trevenant" name="Trevenant" image="/images/trevenant-lor.webp" illus="Yuya Oka" comp="casual"}

### BASIC STRATEGY

Setup and chain Trevenant which has the Old Tree Barrier Ability, which prevents your opponent from taking any Prize cards if it is Knocked Out by one of their Pokémon V. Use Cherrim’s Spring Bloom Ability to power up Trevenant’s Giga Impact attack, which does 150 damage.

### KEY CARDS

- [Trevenant LOR 17](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/jp/S11/12?translate=en)
- [Cherrim BST 8](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/BST/8)
- [Klara](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/CRE/145)

### OTHER CONSIDERATIONS

- [Turffield Stadium](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/RCL/170) as a means of searching for your Evolution Grass-type Pokémon, Trevenant and Cherrim.
- [Energy Recycler](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/BST/124) or [Energy Retrieval](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/SSH/160) to guarantee the continued availability of energy to keep up powering up your chain of Trevenant.
- [Raihan](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/EVS/152) as an out to specific cards in your deck, like Training Court or Cherrim, to make it easier to chain Trevenant.
- [Boss’s Orders](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/BRS/132) to gust up specific Pokémon on your opponent’s Bench to take Prize cards on support Pokémon and on damaged Pokémon.

::::deck{id="swimfreely" name="Swim Freely" image="/images/swim-freely.webp" illus="Taira Akitsu" comp="casual"}

### BASIC STRATEGY

Evolve Seel into Dewgong and use its Floe Return attack, which does 40 damage for each Water Energy attached to your Pokémon that you shuffle back into your deck. To power up Floe Return, flood your hand with Water Energy using Lady, Capacious Bucket, and Nessa, and then use Finneon’s Oceanic Accompaniment Ability, which lets you attach Water Energy from your hand as often as you like to one of your Pokémon with the Swim Freely attack.

### KEY CARDS

- [Dewgong LOR 34](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/jp/S11/21?translate=en)
- [Finneon LOR 40](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/jp/S11/27?translate=en)
- [Irida](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/en/ASR/147)
- Energy search and retrieval cards ([Lady](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/FLI/109), [Capacious Bucket](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/RCL/156), [Nessa](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/VIV/157), [Training Court](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/RCL/169))

### OTHER CONSIDERATIONS

- Use [Zoroark EVS 103](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/EVS/103) to help chain Dewgong.
- Use [Eldegoss EVS 16](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/EVS/16) to make it easier to consistently have Water Energy to attach with Finneon’s Ability.
- [Pyukumuku FST 77](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/FST/77) to draw additional cards.
