With the arrival of Surging Sparks in November, we’ll have a handful of new decks joining the fray. As always, some decks are better than others—some are meme tier and some are merely playable, fun decks without much competitive promise. Lists below are very rough drafts and are intended as a starting place for testing, not as tournament-ready deck lists. Consider [creating and using proxies to test cards you don’t already own](/guide/proxies) prior to making any purchases.
