With the arrival of Paldea Evolved this June, we’ll have a handful of new deck archetypes joining the fray. As always, some deck archetypes are better than others—some are meme tier and some are merely playable, fun decks without much competitive promise. Lists below are very rough drafts and are intended as a starting place for testing, not as tournament-ready deck lists. Consider [creating and using proxies to test cards you don’t already own](/guide/proxies) prior to making any purchases.

    <h1>Meowscarada ex</h1>
    <div class="comp1">
     <span></span>
    </div>
</div>
</div><div class="jb-row"><div class="jb-col-6">

![](/images/e34fa8dcea86ed8d.webp)

*Illus. 5ban Graphics*



### BASIC STRATEGY

Play down and evolve Sprigatito into Meowscarada with the help of Rare Candy. Using Meowscarada ex’s Magic Bouquet Ability, discard a Grass Energy to put 3 damage counters on 1 of your opponent’s benched Pokémon. As needed, use Radiant Alakazam’s Painful Spoons Ability to move damage counters to your opponent’s Active Pokémon to boost the damage of Meowscarada ex’s Nail Scratch attack, which does 100 damage plus 120 more if the Defending Pokémon has any damage on it.

### KEY CARDS

- [Meowscarada ex PAL 15](/highlights/sv2#block-yui_3_17_2_1_1675437289553_38517)
- [Radiant Alakazam SIT 59](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/SIT/59)

### OTHER CONSIDERATIONS

- [Medicham V EVS 83](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/EVS/83) to make use of its Yoga Loop attack to get in extra turns after wearing down your opponent’s Pokémon.
- [Hisuian Lilligant VSTAR ASR 18](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/ASR/18) to take advantage of its Star Perfume VSTAR Power which reads, “During your turn, you may search your deck for up to 5 in any combination of [G] Pokémon and [G] Energy cards, reveal them, and put them into your hand. Then, shuffle your deck. <em>(You can't use more than 1 VSTAR Power in a game.)</em>“
- [Arceus VSTAR BRS 123](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/BRS/123) to take advantage of its Starbirth VSTAR Power which reads, “During your turn, you may search your deck for up to 2 cards and put them into your hand. Then, shuffle your deck. <em>(You can't use more than 1 VSTAR Power in a game.)”</em>
- Building the deck as part of the Lost Zone Box archetype.

### BASIC STRATEGY

Play down and evolve Fuecoco into Skeledirge ex, using a combination of manual attachments, Magma Basin, and Raihan to power up its Burning Voice attack, which does 270 damage minus 10 damage for each damage counter on Skeledirge ex.

### KEY CARDS

- [Skeledirge ex PAL 37](/highlights/sv2#block-yui_3_17_2_1_1675437289553_39949)
- [Magma Basin](https://www.limitlesstcg.com/cards/BRS/144)

### OTHER CONSIDERATIONS

- [Oricorio PAL 33](/highlights/sv2#block-yui_3_17_2_1_1676481203658_26388) to use its Passionate Dance Ability which reads, “Once during your turn, you may heal 20 damage from your Active Evolution Pokémon.” to help heal off damage caused by Magma Basin and the pings of opposing Pokémon, like Hawlucha, Ting-Lu ex, or Meowscarada ex.
- [Armarouge SVI 41](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/SVI/41) to make use of it as a means of funneling Energy to your Skeledirge ex without that Pokémon being damaged by the effect of Magma Basin.
- [Radiant Charizard PGO 11](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/PGO/11) in place of Radiant Greninja for use as a devastating late game single prize attacker.
- [Lumineon V BRS 40](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/BRS/40) for the additional boost to consistency that access to its Luminous Sign Ability brings.

    <h1>Quaquaval ex</h1>
    <div class="comp0">
     <span></span>

![](/images/66e72206a9c900df.webp)

*Illus. 5ban Graphics*



### BASIC STRATEGY

Evolve Quaxly into Quaquaval and Quaquaval ex. Use Quaquaval’s Energy Carnival Ability as necessary to assist in powering up Quaquaval ex’s Propeller Shot attack, which does 230 damage and puts 2 Energy attached to your attacking Pokémon back into your hand. Alternatively, use Quaquaval ex’s Lively Samba attack to switch Quaquaval ex to the bench and put Klefki into the Active Spot to cripple your opponent’s access to their Pokémon’s Abilities.

### KEY CARDS

- [Quaquaval ex PAL 52](/translations/sv2#block-yui_3_17_2_1_1678987201027_397156)
- [Quaquaval SVI 54](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/jp/SVAW/4?translate=en)
- [Klefki SVI 96](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/jp/SV1V/45?translate=en)

### OTHER CONSIDERATIONS

- [Drapion V LOR 118](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/LOR/118) as a counter to Mew VMAX that, with the help of Quaquaval’s Energy Carnival Ability, can also sometimes serve as a counter to Gardevoir ex.
- [Origin Forme Palkia VSTAR 40](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/ASR/40) or [Suicune V EVS 31](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/EVS/31) as alternative attackers.

     <span></span>

![](/images/d1996fb3ffacea1c.webp)

*Illus. aky CG Works*



### BASIC STRATEGY

Play down and evolve Frigibax into Baxcalibur to make use of Baxcalibur’s Super Cold Ability, which reads, “As often as you like during your turn, you may attach a [W] Energy card from your hand to 1 of your Pokémon.” Flood your board with Water Energy, pulling Energy from your deck with Chien-Pao’s Shivery Chill Ability, which reads, “Once during your turn, if this Pokémon is in the Active Spot, you may search your deck for up to 2 basic [W] Energy cards, reveal them, and put them into your hand. Then, shuffle your deck.” Use Chien Pao’s Hail Blade, which does 60 damage for each [W] Energy you discard from your Pokémon, to take Knock Outs.

### KEY CARDS

- [Chien-Pao ex PAL 61](/highlights/sv2#block-yui_3_17_2_1_1679057807143_615473)
- [Baxcalibur PAL 60](/highlights/sv2#block-yui_3_17_2_1_1679057807143_106383)
- [Superior Energy Retrieval](/highlights/sv2#block-yui_3_17_2_1_1675437289553_70613)

### OTHER CONSIDERATIONS

- [Kyogre CRZ 36](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/CRZ/36) to use its Dynamic Wave attack to deal 180 damage to 1 of your opponent’s Pokémon.
- [Wishiwashi EVS 46](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/EVS/46) to serve as a bulky single prize attacker that, with 3 or more Water Energy and a Charm of Courage attached, has 230HP!
- [Luminous Energy](/highlights/sv2#block-yui_3_17_2_1_1675437289553_127880) to still serve as the Metal Energy in Kyurem’s attack cost while still being an Energy that can be discarded to add 60 damage to the attacks of both Chien-Pao ex and Kyurem.
- [Origin Forme Palkia VSTAR 40](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/ASR/40) both as an alternate attacker and as a means of getting two Energy from the discard pile very quickly back into play via its Star Portal VSTAR Power.

    <h1>United Wings</h1>
    <div class="comp0">
     <span></span>
    </div>
</div>
</div><div class="jb-row"><div class="jb-col-6">

![](/images/f69e4339ff664be4.webp)

*Illus. Shibuzoh*



### BASIC STRATEGY

Using every discarding resource in the deck, put all or most of your “United Wings” attackers in the discard pile. Then, attach Energy to Ditto to use its Sudden Transformation Ability to copy the United Wings attack from your discarded “United Wings” attackers. As necessary, use Rescue Carrier to recover a specific United Wings attacker to hit for Weakness against your opponent’s Pokémon.

### KEY CARDS

- United Wings attackers ([Wattrel](/translations/sv2#block-yui_3_17_2_1_1678987201027_803089), [Murkrow](/translations/sv2#block-yui_3_17_2_1_1679638299058_1918286), [Flamigo](/translations/sv2#block-yui_3_17_2_1_1679638299058_2195545))
- [Squawkabilly ex PAL 169](/translations/sv2#block-yui_3_17_2_1_1679315587130_4476139)

### OTHER CONSIDERATIONS
