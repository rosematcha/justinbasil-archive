With the arrival of Crown Zenith this January, we’ll have a handful of new deck archetypes joining the fray. As always, some deck archetypes are better than others—some are meme tier and some are merely playable, fun decks without much competitive promise. Lists below are very rough drafts and are intended as a starting place for testing, not as tournament-ready deck lists. Consider [creating and using proxies to test cards you don’t already own](/guide/proxies) prior to making any purchases.

### BASIC STRATEGY

Discard Pokémon Tool cards with the deck’s various options: Rotom VSTAR’s Conversion Star Ability, Galarian Articuno V’s Reconstitute Ability, Professor’s Research, Serena, Quick Ball, Ultra Ball, and Tower of Darkness. Power up Rotom VSTAR’s Scrap Pulse attack which does 80 damage plus 40 more for each Pokémon Tool card you put into the Lost Zone from your discard pile.

### KEY CARDS

- [Rotom V LOR 58](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/LOR/58)
- [Rotom VSTAR CRZ 46](/highlights/ss125#block-yui_3_17_2_1_1668180913535_107609)

### OTHER CONSIDERATIONS

- [Cramorant LOR 50](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/LOR/50) as an alternate attacker, especially to help deal with lower HP Pokémon, like any single prize Pokémon your opponent puts into the Active Spot to try to flip the prize trade.
- [Porygon2 LOR 141](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/LOR/141) as a late-game attacker that can take the place of Rotom VSTAR when the deck’s supply of Pokémon Tool cards is exhausted.
- [Roxanne](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/ASR/150) to disrupt your opponent’s hand in the mid to late game.

::::deck{id="srcalyrexvmax" name="Shadow Rider Calyrex VMAX with Radiant Eternatus" image="/images/shadow-rider.webp" illus="5ban Graphics" comp="low"}

### BASIC STRATEGY

Bench and evolve Shadow Rider Calyrex V into Shadow Rider Calyrex VMAX to make full use of its Underworld Door ability, which not only attaches a Psychic Energy from your hand to one of your benched Psychic Pokémon, it draws two cards when you do. Use either Shadow Rider Calyrex VMAX’s Max Geist attack or Alcremie VMAX’s G-Max Whisk attack to take knockouts.

### KEY CARDS

- [Shadow Rider Calyrex VMAX CRE 75](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/CRE/75)
- [Alcremie VMAX CPA 23](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/cpa/23/)
- [Fog Crystal](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/CRE/140)

### OTHER CONSIDERATIONS

- A copy of [Energy Recycler](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/BST/124) for an additional means of recovering the deck’s basic Psychic Energy.

::::deck{id="zacianvstar" name="Zacian VSTAR" image="/images/zacian-vstar-5f2baf.webp" illus="5ban Graphics" comp="low"}

### BASIC STRATEGY

Using Metal Saucer, Zacian’s *Intrepid Sword* ability, and manual attachments, get Zacian V’s Brave Blade attack powered up as quickly as possible. Use the deck’s various switching options to reset the effect of Brave Blade or evolve into Zacian VSTAR both to boost Zacian’s HP and to instead use its Break Edge or Star Sword attacks, which do 200 damage that cuts through any effects on the Defending Pokémon and 310 damage with 30 recoil damage, respectively.

### KEY CARDS

- [Zacian V SSH 138](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/SSH/138)
- [Zacian VSTAR CRZ 96](/highlights/ss125#block-yui_3_17_2_1_1668180913535_112479)
- Switching Cards ([Bird Keeper](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/DAA/159), [Cross Switcher](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/FST/230), [Scoop Up Net](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/RCL/165), [Air Balloon](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/SSH/156))
- [Metal Saucer](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/SSH/170)

### OTHER CONSIDERATIONS

- [Zamazenta V SSH 139](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/SSH/139) as a wall against Pokémon VMAX and [Zamazenta VSTAR](/highlights/ss125#block-yui_3_17_2_1_1668180913535_117833) to instead use its Sheild Star VSTAR Power, which reduces the damage taken from your opponent’s Pokémon’s attacks by all of your Pokémon in play by 100 during your opponent’s next turn.
- [Echoing Horn](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/CRE/136) to re-bench more vulnerable Pokémon V from your opponent’s discard pile to gust them up with Serena to knock them out.

::::deck{id="regigigasvstar" name="Regigigas VSTAR" image="/images/regigigas-vstar-0179e8.webp" illus="PLANETA Mochizuki" comp="low"}

### BASIC STRATEGY

Play down Regigigas V and Sobble, then evolve Sobble into Drizzile to take advantage of its Shady Dealings Ability, which lets you search your deck for a Trainer card to put into your hand. Evolve Regigigas V into Regigigas VSTAR and use a combination of Melony and Double Turbo Energy to power up its Giga Imapact attack, which does 230 damage. Reduce the damage taken by Regigigas VSTAR with the decks various damage reduction options. Scoop Regigigas VSTAR back into your hand to avoid it giving up Prize cards with Cheren’s Care.

### KEY CARDS

- Inteleon Engine ([Sobble CRE 41](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/CRE/41), [Drizzile SSH 56](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/SSH/56), [Inteleon SSH 58](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/SSH/58), [Irida](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/ASR/147), [Scoop Up Net](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/RCL/165))
- [Regigigas V CRZ 113](https://www.pokebeach.com/2022/11/new-vstar-universe-cards-revealed-including-hatterene-vmax)
- [Regigigas VSTAR CRZ 114](/highlights/ss125#block-yui_3_17_2_1_1668180913535_166843)
- [Melony](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/CRE/146)
- Damage Reduction, Healing, and HP Boost Effects ([Radiant Gardevoir LOR 69](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/LOR/69), [Cheren’s Care](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/BRS/134), [Big Charm](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/SSH/158), [V Guard Energy](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/SIT/169))

### OTHER CONSIDERATIONS

- [Inteleon CRE 43](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/CRE/43) to make use of its Quick Shooting Ability to set up Knock Outs.
- [Forest Seal Stone](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/SIT/156) to give the deck an out to a significantly better VSTAR Power than that printed on Regigigas VSTAR.
- [Big Parasol](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/DAA/157) to shield Regigigas from the effect of [Yveltal](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/SHF/46)’s Amazing Destruction attack.
