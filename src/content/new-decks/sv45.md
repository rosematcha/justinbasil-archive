With the arrival of Paldean Fates in January, we’ll have a handful of new deck archetypes joining the fray. As always, some deck archetypes are better than others—some are meme tier and some are merely playable, fun decks without much competitive promise. Lists below are very rough drafts and are intended as a starting place for testing, not as tournament-ready deck lists. Consider [creating and using proxies to test cards you don’t already own](/guide/proxies) prior to making any purchases.

    <h1>Espathra ex</h1>
    <div class="comp0">
     <span></span>
    </div>

![](/images/0d5082487cbe8c7a.webp)

*Illus. takuyoa*



### BASIC STRATEGY

Play down and evolve Natu into Xatu, using Xatu’s Clairvoyant Sense Ability to attach a Psychic Energy card from your hand to 1 of your Benched Pokémon and draw two cards. Attack using Espathra ex’s Psy Ball attack, which does 30 damage plus 30 more damage for each Energy attached to both Active Pokémon, all while Espathra’s Ability, Dazzling Gaze, and Pokémon League Headquarters (against Basic Pokémon) make it cost [C] more each for their Pokémon to attack.

### KEY CARDS

- [Espathra ex PAF](/translations/sv45#block-yui_3_17_2_1_1700799489795_99692)
- [Xatu PAR 72](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/PAR/72)

### OTHER CONSIDERATIONS

- [Tulip](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/PAR/181) for Pokémon and recovery of Basic Psychic Energy, which can also mean you’re able to use Xatu’s Clairvoyant Sense Ability to draw cards more often.

    <h1>United Wings</h1>
    <div class="comp0">

    </div>

![](/images/00c8ecd5d68dbf5b.webp)

*Illus. AKIRA EGAWA*



### BASIC STRATEGY

Using every discarding resource in the deck, put all or most of your “United Wings” attackers in the discard pile. Then, attach Energy to Ditto to use its Sudden Transformation Ability to copy the United Wings attack from your discarded “United Wings” attackers. As necessary, use Rescue Carrier to recover a specific United Wings attacker to hit for Weakness against your opponent’s Pokémon.

### KEY CARDS

- United Wings attackers ([Flamigo PAL 170](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/PAL/170), [Murkrow PAL 131](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/PAL/131), and [Wattrel PAL 80](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/PAL/80))
- [Ditto CRZ 107](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/CRZ/107)
- [Squawkabilly ex PAL 169](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/PAL/169)

### OTHER CONSIDERATIONS

- Forgoing the Kilowattrel in favor of an additional consistency card, like a second copy of Squawkabilly ex.

    <h1>Great Tusk ex</h1>
    <div class="comp0">

![](/images/617ff2e9b1752600.webp)

*Illus. kawayoo*



### BASIC STRATEGY

Build up Great Tusk ex on the Bench with the help of Professor Sada’s Vitality, then, with Path to the Peak in play, switch Great Tusk ex into the Active Spot and attack with its Great Bash attack, which does 260 damage and is unaffected by any effects on your opponent’s Active Pokémon.

### KEY CARDS

- [Great Tusk ex PAF 53](/translations/sv45#block-yui_3_17_2_1_1699082920675_422634)
- [Professor Sada’s Vitality](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/PAR/170)
- [Energy Switch](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/SVI/173)
- [Path to the Peak](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/CRE/148)

### OTHER CONSIDERATIONS

- [Koraidon ex SVI 125](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/SVI/125) to take advantage of its Dino Cry Ability to help accelerate Fighting Energy into play, especially on turn 1.
- [Squawkabilly ex PAL 169](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/PAL/169) to churn through more of your deck on your first turn, to help guarantee getting a Great Bash on turn 1 going second.
- [Grant](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/ASR/144) to boost the damage of Great Tusk ex’s Great Bash by 30, as needed.

    <h1>Mabosstiff ex</h1>
    <div class="comp0">
     <span></span>
    </div>
</div>
</div><div class="jb-row"><div class="jb-col-6">

![](/images/2641c3b5a0e62555.webp)

*Illus. PLANETA Mochizuki*



### BASIC STRATEGY

After evolving Maschiff into Mabosstiff ex, use a combination of manual attachments of Double Turbo Energy and Dark Patch to power up Mabosstiff ex’s Proud Fangs attack, which does 100 damage plus 120 more damage if any of your Benched Pokémon have damage counters on them. To guarantee that you have a Pokémon with damage counters on your bench, discard Gengar with Trekking Shoes, Ultra Ball, or Professor’s Research and then use Gengar’s Netherword Gate Ability which pulls Gengar out of the discard pile and puts 3 damage counters on it.

### KEY CARDS

- [Mabosstiff ex SVP 86](/translations/misc#block-yui_3_17_2_1_1701966740928_9826)
- [Dark Patch](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/ASR/139)
- [Double Turbo Energy](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/BRS/151)

### OTHER CONSIDERATIONS

- An additional copy each of Iono and Path to the Peak and further hand disruption options, like [Judge](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/SVI/176).

    <h1>Iron Treads ex</h1>
    <div class="comp0">
     <span></span>
    </div>
</div>
</div><div class="jb-row"><div class="jb-col-6">

![](/images/bcb5bf218c1c2b46.webp)

*Illus. toriyufu*



### BASIC STRATEGY

Evolve up Magnemite into Magnezone via Rare Candy and use its Giga Magnet Ability to power up Iron Treads ex’s Clad Rolling attack, which does 150 damage and, if you have a Future Booster Energy Capsule attached to Iron Treads ex, reduces the damage it takes during your opponent’s next turn by 150, effectively giving Iron Treads ex 370 HP.

### KEY CARDS

- [Iron Treads ex PAF 66](/translations/sv45#block-yui_3_17_2_1_1699082920675_483991)
- [Magnezone ASR 107](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/ASR/107)
- [Miraidon ex SVI 81](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/SVI/81)

### OTHER CONSIDERATIONS

- [Technical Machine: Turbo Energize](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/PAR/179) to attach two Basic Energy cards from your deck to 1 of your Pokémon and [Bronzong BST 102](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/BST/102) to take advantage of its Metal Transfer Ability to move Metal Energy around your Pokémon as you please. Potentially also a copy or two of [Penny](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/SVI/183), to scoop up a damaged Iron Treads ex after rearranging the energy.
