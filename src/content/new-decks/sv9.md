As always, some decks are better than others—some are meme tier and some are merely playable, fun decks without much competitive promise. Lists below are very rough drafts and are intended as a starting place for testing, not as tournament-ready deck lists. Consider [creating and using proxies to test cards you don’t already own](/guide/proxies) prior to making any purchases.

::::deck{id="festivalgrounds" name="Festival Grounds" image="/images/festival-grounds-dbb013.webp" illus="MARINA Chikazawa" comp="casual"}

### BASIC STRATEGY

Play down and evolve Grookey and Applin into Thwackey and Dipplin, respectively. With the Festival Grounds stadium card in play, attack with Dipplin’s Do the Wave attack, which does 20 damage for each of your Benched Pokémon. Then, because of the effect of Dipplin’s Festival Lead Ability, attack once again before your opponent starts their turn. With Dipplin in the Active Spot, make use of Thwackey’s Boom Boom Groove Ability, which lets you search your deck for any 1 card if your Active Pokémon has the Festival Lead Ability.

### BASIC STRATEGY

Pile damage counters on Pokémon in play with Abilities, except for Froslass, with Froslass’ Freezing Shroud Ability which reads, “During Pokémon Checkup, put 1 damage counter on each Pokémon that has an Ability <em>(both yours and your opponent's)</em>, except any Froslass.” Use Munkidori’s Adrena-Brain Ability (Once during your turn, if this Pokémon has any [D] Energy attached, you may move up to 3 damage counters from 1 of your Pokémon to 1 of your opponent's Pokémon.) to move generated damage counters off your Pokémon onto your opponent’s Pokémon, gradually spreading damage on their board. As necessary, use Hisuian Arcanine or Bloodmoon Ursaluna ex to finish up knockouts.
