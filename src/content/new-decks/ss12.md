With the arrival of Lost Origin this September, we’ll have a handful of new deck archetypes joining the fray. As always, some deck archetypes are better than others—some are meme tier and some are merely playable, fun decks without much competitive promise. Lists below are very rough drafts and are intended as a starting place for testing, not as tournament-ready deck lists. Consider [creating and using proxies to test cards you don’t already own](/guide/proxies) prior to making any purchases.

    <h2>Sunflora</h2>
    <div class="compm">
     <span></span>
    </div>
</div>
</div><div class="jb-row"><div class="jb-col-6">

![](/images/sunflora-lor.webp)

*Illus. zig*



### BASIC STRATEGY

Bench Gossifleur, Zorua, and Sunkern, then evolve Sunkern into Sunflora, Gossifleur into Eldegoss, and Zorua into Zoroark. Attack using Sunflora’s Brilliant Beam attack, which does 10 damage plus 70 more damage for each Energy card you discard from your hand—up to 3. As Sunflora are Knocked Out, make use of Zoroark’s Phantom Transformation Ability to swap the Zoroark with a Sunflora in your discard pile. Use Eldegoss’ Cotton Lift Ability to put two Energy cards from your deck into your hand each turn to help guarantee maximum damage on Sunflora’s attack.

### KEY CARDS

- [Sunflora SIT 6](https://www.serebii.net/card/lostabyss/004.shtml)
- [Zoroark EVS 103](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/EVS/103)

### OTHER CONSIDERATIONS

- [Mightyena ASR 96](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/ASR/96) as an option to help counter prominent Darkness-weak archetypes, like Mew VMAX and Shadow Rider Calyrex VMAX.
- [Slowbro PGO 20](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/PGO/20) to make it easier to wrap up a game if you slip a bit in the Prize trade.
- [Cherrim BST 8](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/BST/8) and Grass Energy in place of Water Energy to make the deck less reliant on Twin Energy, allowing you to push more heavily in the direction of mostly or entirely all basic Energy, which are both easier to recover and to attach.
- [Nessa](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/VIV/157) as an option to recover both Radiant Greninja and basic Water Energy.

    <h2>Serperior VSTAR</h2>
    <div class="comp1">
     <span></span>

![](/images/serperior-vstar-2709d8.webp)

*Illus. PLANETA Mochizuki*



### BASIC STRATEGY

Evolve Serperior V into Serperior VSTAR to prepare it to attack and Kricketot into Kricketune to take advantage of its Swelling Tune Ability, which reads, “Your [G] Pokémon in play, except any Kricketune, get +40 HP. You can't apply more than 1 Swelling Tune Ability at a time.” Further increase the difficulty of knocking out your own Serperior VSTAR with Big Charm and Radiant Gardevoir whose ability reduces the damage done to your Pokémon by Pokémon V by 20. Attack using Serperior VSTAR’s Regal Blender attack, which does 190 damage and gives you the opportunity to move any Energy cards attach to your Pokémon to your other Pokémon in any way you like. When advantageous, use Serperior VSTAR’s Star Winder attack, which does 60 damage for each Energy card attached to Serperior VSTAR.

### KEY CARDS

- [Serperior VSTAR SIT 8](/highlights/ss12#block-yui_3_17_2_1_1660315435813_50675)
- [Cheryl](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/BST/123)

### OTHER CONSIDERATIONS

- 1 copy of [Pokémon Breeder’s Nuturing](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/DAA/166) to make it easier to get both Kricketune and Serperior VSTAR into play on the same turn.
- [Gardenia’s Vigor](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/ASR/143) as an option to draw a couple of cards while also attaching any up to 2 Grass Energy you draw into or have in your hand to 1 of your Benched Pokémon.
- [Turffield Stadium](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/RCL/170) as an additional means of finding Kricketune and Serperior VSTAR.
- [Altaria SIT 143](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/jp/S11a/57?translate=en) to make use of its Magical Echo attack, which can be used to move all damage counters from 1 of your heavily-damaged Serperior VSTAR to your opponent’s Active Pokémon, potentially with 1 copy of [Altaria EVS 106](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/EVS/106) to be able to take advantage of its Tempting Tune Ability, which reads, “Once during your turn, you may search your deck for a Supporter card, reveal it, shuffle your deck, then put that card on top of it.”

    <h2>Braixen</h2>
    <div class="comp0">
     <span></span>

![](/images/braixen-sit.webp)

*Illus. Ligton*



### BASIC STRATEGY

Bench Espurr and Fennekin and evolve Fennekin into Braixen so that you can attack with its Flare Parade attack, which does 60 damage for each Serena card in your discard pile. To get Serena in the discard pile, use Meowstic’s on-evolution ability, Inviting Ear, which lets you search your deck for any two Supporter cards and put them into your hand. Play Serena itself, which has you discard up to three cards from your hand and then draw until you have five cards in your hand, or otherwise discard Serena with the decks many discarding options, including Ultra Ball, Quick Ball, Professor’s Research, and PokéStop.

### KEY CARDS

- [Braixen SIT 26](/highlights/ss12#block-yui_3_17_2_1_1659085811524_439715)
- [Meowstic SIT 82](/highlights/ss12#block-yui_3_17_2_1_1659085811524_129914)

### OTHER CONSIDERATIONS

- Running the [Kirlia](/highlights/ss12#block-yui_3_17_2_1_1659085811524_272245) engine (4 [Ralts SIT 67](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/jp/S11a/36?translate=en), 3-4 [Kirlia SIT 68](/highlights/ss12#block-yui_3_17_2_1_1659085811524_272245), 0-1 [Kirlia CRE 60](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/CRE/60)) with 1-2 copies of [Gallade ASR 62](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/ASR/62).
- [Trekking Shoes](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/ASR/156) to quickly burn through the deck to find Serena faster.
- [Mew CEL 21](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/CEL/11) as an additional out to critical Item cards, like Rescue Carrier.
- [Energy Loto](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/ASR/140) to dig futher to find the deck’s Special Energy cards, [Roseanne’s Backup](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/BRS/148) or [Cyllene](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/ASR/138) to recover them.

*Illus. Souichirou Gunjima*



### BASIC STRATEGY

Attack using the various pikaclones in order, beginning with Togedemaru whose Togedash attack does 10 damage and if you get a heads on the coin flip, prevents your opponent from taking a Prize card if it is Knocked Out during your opponent’s next turn. Continue the chain with Dedenne’s Dedeshort, which does 60 damage and Paralyzes your opponent’s Active Pokémon. Further continue the chain with either Pikachu’s Pikastrike attack which does 200 damage if your Dedenne attacked the previous turn or with Morpeko’s Pekobomber, which does 60 damage to all of your opponent’s Pokémon if your Dedenne attacked during your previous turn.

### KEY CARDS

- [Togedemaru SIT 127](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/jp/S12/69?translate=en)
- [Dedenne SIT 85](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/jp/S12/45?translate=en)

- [Morpeko SIT 116](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/jp/S12/62?translate=en)

### OTHER CONSIDERATIONS

- [Radiant Alakazam](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/jp/S11a/31?translate=en) as an option to redistribute spread damage counters to better set up Knock Outs against your opponent’s Pokémon.

![](/images/regieleki-vmax.webp)

*Illus. N Design Inc.*



### BASIC STRATEGY

Play down Vikavolt V and attach either a basic Lightning Energy or a Speed Lightning Energy to it. After discarding a basic Water Energy with any of the deck’s various discarding options, use Melony to attach the Water Energy to Vikavolt and attack using its Paralyzing Bolt attack, which does 50 damage and locks your opponent out of the use of any Item cards during their next turn. Whilst preparing your Vikavolt V to attack, bench and evolve Regieleki V into Regieleki VMAX to benefit from its Transistor Ability, which boosts the damage of the attacks of your basic Lightning Pokémon by 30—an ability that stacks.

### KEY CARDS

- [Vikavolt V DAA 60](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/en/DAA/60)
- [Regieleki VMAX SIT 58](https://www.pokebeach.com/2022/09/regidrago-vstar-regieleki-vmax-omastar-v-and-more-from-paradigm-trigger)
- [Speed Lightning Energy](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/RCL/173)

### OTHER CONSIDERATIONS

- [Raihan](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/EVS/152) as alternative to Melony for situations in which a Water Energy is already attached to a Vikavolt V, but a Lightning Energy is needed. Also useful as an out to basically any non-Supporter card in deck, including Choice Band.
- [Raikou V BRS 48](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/BRS/48) as an alternate attacker that can ramp the damage of its two-energy attack faster than Vikavolt V.
- [Double Turbo Energy](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/BRS/151) to make it easier to attack with Regieleki VMAX, if necessary.
- [Training Court](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/RCL/169) as an option to recover Lightning Energy.

    <h2>Gardevoir</h2>
    <div class="comp0">
     <span></span>

</div><div class="jb-row"><div class="jb-col-6">

![](/images/gardevoir-cre.webp)

*Illus. kodama*



### BASIC STRATEGY

Play down a Ralts and evolve it into a the Kirlia from Chilling Reign. Use its Mirage Step attack which reads, “Search your deck for up to 3 Kirlia and put them onto your Bench. Then, shuffle your deck.” Use that attack to get your Kirlia with the Refinement Ability into play and either evolve your Mirage Step Kirlia into Gallade to use its Swirling Slice attack, which does 160 damage and has you move an Energy attached to it to 1 of your Benched Pokémon. Alternatively, evolve the Kirlia into Gardevoir and use its Brainwave attack, which does 60 damage plus 30 more damage for each Psychic Energy attached to the attacking Gardevoir. Get additional Energy into play using Raihan, and, when the opportunity strikes, make use of Radiant Charizard’s Excited Heart Ability to sharply reduce the attack cost of Combustion Blast, which does 250 damage before any damage modifiers.

### KEY CARDS

- [Gardevoir CRE 61](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/CRE/61)
- [Kirlia SIT 68](/highlights/ss12#block-yui_3_17_2_1_1659085811524_272245)
- [Gallade ASR 62](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/ASR/62)

### OTHER CONSIDERATIONS

- [Cresselia CRE 64](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/CRE/64) both to take advantage of its Crescent Glow attack to help set up energy to boost the damage of your Gardevoir’s attacks and to use its own Photon Laser attack in case of emergencies.
- [Galarian Articuno EVS 63](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/EVS/63) as a basic Pokémon that can both boost Gardevoir’s damage via its Cruel Charge Ability and can snipe your opponent’s weakened or weak Benched Pokémon with its Psylaser attack, which does 120 damage to 1 of your opponent’s Pokémon.

    <h2>Regidrago VSTAR</h2>
    <div class="comp2">
     <span></span>
    </div>
</div>
</div><div class="jb-row"><div class="jb-col-6">

![](/images/regidrago-vstar.webp)

*Illus. PLANETA Yamashita*



### BASIC STRATEGY

Discard your non-Regidrago Dragon-typed Pokémon. Bench and evolve Regidrago V into Regidrago VSTAR, then use its Apex Dragon attack, which lets you copy the attack of any Dragon-type Pokémon in your discard pile for the cost of two Grass Energy and one Fire Energy. When possible, attack copying Dragonite’s Energy Hurricane attack, which does 180 damage and lets you search your deck for 3 Basic Energy cards and attach them to your Pokémon in any way you like. Continue copying other the attacks of discarded Dragon-type Pokémon as beneficial: attack using Goodra’s Rolling Iron to do 200 damage and take 80 less during your opponent’s next turn, attack using Giratina VSTAR’s Lost Impact which does 280 damage and you send two Energy attached to your Pokémon to the Lost Zone.

### KEY CARDS

- [Regidrago VSTAR SIT 125](https://www.pokebeach.com/2022/09/regidrago-vstar-regieleki-vmax-omastar-v-and-more-from-paradigm-trigger)
- [Dragonite SIT 131](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/jp/S12/72?translate=en)
- [Giratina VSTAR LOR 131](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/LOR/131)
- [Hisuian Goodra VSTAR LOR 136](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/LOR/136)

### OTHER CONSIDERATIONS

- An alternate engine in place of the Lost engine, using [Gardenia’s Vigor](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/ASR/143) and either the Inteleon, Mew, Bibarel, or Kirlia engines to support the deck in finding the cards it needs to operate.
- [Duraludon VMAX EVS 123](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/EVS/123) to give Regidrago VSTAR the option to copy its G-Max Pulverization attack which does 220 damage and isn’t affected by any effects on your opponent's Active Pokémon.
- [Garchomp V ASR 117](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/ASR/117) to give Regidrago VSTAR the option to copy its Sonic Strike attack, which reads, “Discard 3 Energy from this Pokémon. This attack does 220 damage to 1 of your opponent's Pokémon. <em>(Don't apply Weakness and Resistance for Benched Pokémon.)</em>”
- [Choice Belt](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/BRS/135) to boost Regidrago VSTAR’s damage output, to help it hit key numbers when attacking with Hisuian Goodra VSTAR’s Rolling Star attack.
- [Flygon V BRS 106](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/BRS/106) to give Regidrago VSTAR the option to copy its Draconic Impulse attack, which reads, “If your opponent's Active Pokémon is a Pokémon VMAX, this attack does 160 more damage, and discard 3 Energy from this Pokémon.”

*Illus. Mitsuhiro Arita*



### BASIC STRATEGY

Get two Archeops into the discard pile while benching and eventually evolving Lugia V into Lugia VSTAR. Prioritize getting two Archeops into play using Lugia’s Summoning Star VSTAR Power, which puts two Colorless Pokémon from your discard pile directly onto your bench. Use Archeops’ Primal Turbo Ability and manual attachments to power up Lugia VSTAR’s Tempest Dive attack, which does 220 damage and grants you the option to discard a Stadium card in play. Boost the damage of Tempest dive with Choice Belt and Powerful Colorless Energy, all while making your own Lugia VSTAR more difficult to Knock Out with the help of Radiant Gardevoir’s Loving Veil Ability, which reduces the damage Pokémon V do to your Pokémon by 20, and further reduce the damage Lugia VSTAR takes with V Guard Energy, which reduces the damage Pokémon V do to Lugia by 30.

### KEY CARDS

- [Lugia VSTAR SIT 139](/highlights/ss12#block-yui_3_17_2_1_1663488163133_261232)

- [Lugia VIV 132](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/VIV/132) as a single prize attacking option to abuse its Wind Pressure attack which does 250 base damage if your opponent has 6 or more cards in their hand.
- [Lost Vacuum](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/LOR/162) to deal with tools like Cape of Toughness and Big Charm and hostile Stadium cards, like Collapsed Stadium.

- [Leon](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/VIV/154) to further boost the damage of Tempest Dive.
- [Professor Burnet](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/SSP/167) as a Supporter that can be searched for with Lumineon V’s Luminous Sign Ability to help guarantee the two Archeops make it to the discard pile early in the game.
