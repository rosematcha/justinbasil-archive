With the arrival of Pokémon GO this July, we’ll have a handful of new deck archetypes joining the fray. As always, some deck archetypes are better than others—some are meme tier and some are merely playable, fun decks without much competitive promise. Lists below are very rough drafts and are intended as a starting place for testing, not as tournament-ready deck lists. Consider [creating and using proxies to test cards you don’t already own](/guide/proxies) prior to making any purchases.

::::deck{id="magikarp" name="Magikarp" image="/images/magikarp-pgo.webp" illus="N-Design Inc." comp="low"}

### BASIC STRATEGY

Dump as many Magikarp and Gyarados into the discard pile as you can, as fast as you can, using the deck’s discarding options like Professor’s Research, Cinccino’s Make Do ability, Trekking Shoes, Quick Ball, and Ultra Ball. Use Ditto’s Sudden Transformation ability to copy Magikarp’s Raging Fin attack, which does 10 damage plus 30 more damage for each “Magikarp” and each “Gyarados” in your discard pile.

### KEY CARDS

- [Ditto PGO 53](/highlights/ss105#block-yui_3_17_2_1_1653578873355_80363)
- [Magikarp PGO 21](/highlights/ss105#block-yui_3_17_2_1_1656622908671_33520)

### OTHER CONSIDERATIONS

- [Hisuian Heavy Ball](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/ASR/146) and/or [Peonia](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/CRE/149) as a means of fishing out Magikarp and Gyarados from your Prize cards.
- [Roseanne’s Backup](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/BRS/148) as a means of recovering Twin Energy and Double Turbo energy, Choice Belt, and more.

### BASIC STRATEGY

Play down and evolve Shadow Rider Calyrex VMAX and use its Underworld Door ability to attach Psychic energy to power up your benched Psychic Pokémon, including other Shadow Rider Calyrex VMAX and Mewtwo VSTAR. Attack with Shadow Rider Calyrex VMAX’s Max Geist which does 10 damage plus 30 more damage for every Psychic energy attached to all of your Pokémon or with Mewtwo VSTAR’s Psy Purge attack, which does 90 damage for each Psychic energy card you discard from Mewtwo VSTAR (up to 3, or 270 damage).

### KEY CARDS

- [Shadow Rider Calyrex VMAX CRE 75](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/CRE/75)
- [Mewtwo VSTAR PGO 31](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/PGO/31)

### OTHER CONSIDERATIONS

- Instead of relying on the 3-prize Shadow Rider Calyrex VMAX engine for energy acceleration, make use of the [Lunala](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/PGO/34) and [Solrock](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/PGO/39) engine from Pokémon GO.

::::deck{id="shedinja" name="Shedinja" image="/images/shedinja-42821d.webp" illus="Tomokazu Komiya" comp="casual"}

### BASIC STRATEGY

Dump a Shedinja into the discard pile and use Ditto’s Sudden Transformation ability to copy Shedinja’s Life Squeeze attack, which puts damage counters on your opponent’s active Pokémon until it has 10 HP left. Prior to attacking, use Garbodor’s Poisonous Puddle ability to poison that Pokémon, ensuring that the poison damage knocks it out during Pokémon Checkup.

### KEY CARDS

- [Ditto PGO 53](/highlights/ss105#block-yui_3_17_2_1_1653578873355_80363)
- [Shedinja VIV 66](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/VIV/66)
- [Garbodor RCL 118](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/RCL/118)

### OTHER CONSIDERATIONS

- [Manaphy BRS 41](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/BRS/41) to protect your benched Pokémon.
- [Boss’s Orders](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/BRS/132) or [Cross Switcher](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/FST/230) as alternate gusting options.
- [Crobat V DAA 104](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/DAA/104) or the [Bibarel](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/BRS/121) engine to help the deck draw more cards, more consistently.

::::deck{id="melmetalvmax" name="Melmetal VMAX" image="/images/melmetal-vmax.webp" illus="PLANETA Tsuji" comp="casual"}

### BASIC STRATEGY

Using a combination of Metal Saucer and manual attachments, prepare to attack with Melmetal VMAX’s G-Max Juggernaut attack, which does 160 damage and does up to 120 more damage if you have a total of 5 Metal Energy attached to Melmetal VMAX. As needed, attack using Zacian V’s Brave Blade attack or with Dialga’s Metal Blast attack.

### KEY CARDS

- [Melmetal VMAX PGO 48](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/PGO/48)
- [Zacian V SSH 138](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/SSH/138)

### OTHER CONSIDERATIONS

- [Energy Switch](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/SSH/162) to move energy around as necessary to ensure Melmetal VMAX is able to get maximum value out of its G-Max Juggernaut attack.
- [Diancie ASR 68](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/ASR/68) to protect Melmetal V as you power it up to prepare to evolve it and use Melmetal VMAX to attack.

::::deck{id="snorlax" name="Snorlax" image="/images/snorlax-pgo.webp" illus="N-DESIGN Inc." comp="low"}

### BASIC STRATEGY

Using a combination of Miltank’s Miracle Body ability and Snorlax’s Block ability, prevent your opponent from successfully attacking, using gusting cards like Boss’s Orders, Cross Switcher, and Pokémon Catcher to strand their Pokémon in the Active Spot all whilst removing their energy with Crushing Hammer, Fan of Waves, and Sidney. Drive them closer and closer to deckout using Lure Module and Cursed Shovel.

### KEY CARDS

- [Snorlax PGO 55](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/PGO/55)
- [Miltank ASR 126](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/ASR/126)

### OTHER CONSIDERATIONS

- Additional disruption options, like [Path to the Peak](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/CRE/148) or [Temple of Sinnoh](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/ASR/155).
- Alternate Supporter recovery in the form of [Team Yell’s Cheer](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/BRS/149).

::::deck{id="ambipom" name="Ambipom" image="/images/ambipom-pgo.webp" illus="Scav" comp="casual"}

### BASIC STRATEGY

Evolve Aipom into Ambipom early to abuse its Primate Dexterity ability, which requires you to flip a coin each time your opponent would do damage to Ambipom via an attack—and a heads on that coin flip means that Ambipom takes no damage and survives to the next turn to use its Full Tilt Fling attack, which has you flip a coin for each energy card attached to Ambipom and does 60 damage for each heads you flip. When Ambipom isn’t ready, use Miltank and its Miracle Body ability to wall against Pokémon V to stall for a turn or two.

### KEY CARDS

- [Ambipom PGO 57](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/PGO/57)
- [Miltank ASR 126](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/ASR/126)

### OTHER CONSIDERATIONS

- [Cherrim BST 8](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/BST/8) for its Spring Bloom ability as an alternate means of flooding Ambipom with energy, freeing up the spots that Gardenia’s Vitality takes for stronger draw Supporters, like Marnie or Professor’s Research.
- [Manaphy BRS 41](https://limitlesstcg.com/cards/BRS/41) to protect your benched Pokémon.
