### Trainer Gallery

The set description for *Astral Radiance* mentions that it contains “a special Trainer Gallery subset of 30 cards with artwork featuring fan-favorite Trainers and Pokémon.” However, with the high number of Character Rare (CHR) and Character Super Rare (CSR) cards still remaining, even with this second Trainer Gallery, <strong>not all of the remaining special cards from VMAX Climax will appear in *Astral Radiance*</strong>. To see cards that may be included in future Trainer Galleries, see the [Trainer Galleries article](/visual/tg).
