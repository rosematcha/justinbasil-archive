**Secret Rares**

Beginning with Scarlet & Violet base set, Full Art cards are now also considered secret rares. These cards will have a collector number above that of the number of cards in the set, e.g. 201/198. Additionally, this set features a chunk of cards in its set list that are similar to what would have been made into a Shiny Vault subset in previous “shiny” or “fates” mini sets. Unlike previous “Fates” sets, these cards are number with the rest of the set’s secret rare cards.
